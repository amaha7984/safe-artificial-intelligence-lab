Mission

Capability and safety, studied together

We believe that advancing what AI can do and understanding when it can be trusted are parts of the same research problem.

The Safe Artificial Intelligence Lab is a newly established research group at Purdue University Northwest. Our research explores how to build capable AI systems, how to make them verifiable, robust, and transparent, and how to apply them responsibly where their decisions affect people and communities. We also aim to give students meaningful experience in careful, rigorous AI research.
