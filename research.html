Research

Research Directions

Our research is organized around three interconnected directions that follow a single idea: develop AI, make it safe and trustworthy, and apply it responsibly for society.

  • AI Development
  • AI Safety & Trust
  • AI for Society

Direction 01 · Develop

AI Development

Building advanced intelligent systems

Our research explores how to build capable AI systems: models that generate, perceive, reason, and act. We approach model development with reliability and downstream use in mind from the start.

  • Generative AI

    We investigate generative models, their mathematical foundations, and how their outputs can be controlled and evaluated.

  • Visual & Multimodal Intelligence

    We study models that interpret visual information and connect it with language and other modalities.

  • Agentic & Embodied AI

    We explore AI agents that plan and take actions, including agentic approaches to computer vision tasks.

Direction 02 · Make Safe

AI Safety & Trust

Making AI trustworthy, verifiable, and resilient

As AI systems become more capable, it becomes more important to understand, verify, and secure them. We investigate methods that help establish when AI systems, and the content they produce, can be trusted.

  • AI Forensics & Content Provenance

    We investigate methods for detecting AI-generated content and for tracing the origin and history of digital media.

  • Verification, Evaluation & Explainability

    We explore rigorous ways to evaluate model behavior and to make model decisions easier to inspect and understand.

  • Robustness, Security & Privacy

    We study how AI systems respond to distribution shifts and adversarial manipulation, and how to protect the data they rely on.

Direction 03 · Apply

AI for Society

Advancing AI for responsible and beneficial real-world use

Our research explores how trustworthy AI methods can be applied to real-world problems, and what responsible use requires in practice.

  • Geospatial AI

    We explore AI for remote sensing and geospatial data, where reliability directly affects downstream decisions.

  • Healthcare AI

    We are interested in AI methods for health-related data, where safety, fairness, and interpretability are essential.

  • Responsible & Ethical AI

    We consider fairness, accountability, and transparency as part of how AI systems are designed and used.

  • Critical Applications

    We study the requirements of AI used in high-stakes settings, where errors carry significant consequences.

  • Sustainable AI

    We explore ways to reduce the computational and environmental costs of developing and deploying AI.
