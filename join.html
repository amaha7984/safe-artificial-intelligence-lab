Join Us

Join the Lab

The Safe Artificial Intelligence Lab is a growing research program at Purdue University Northwest. We welcome interest from students and collaborators who want to work toward AI that is capable, trustworthy, and beneficial to society.

Who we would like to hear from

If your interests connect with one of our research directions, we would be glad to hear from you.

  • Undergraduate Students

    Undergraduates curious about AI research who want to learn how research questions are investigated in practice.

  • Graduate Students

    Graduate students interested in research aligned with AI development, AI safety and trust, or AI for society.

  • Doctoral Researchers

    Prospective and current doctoral researchers whose interests align with safe and trustworthy AI.

  • Research Collaborators

    Faculty, researchers, and partners interested in exploring shared research questions.

How to reach out

Any specific openings will be posted on this page.

  1. Review our research directions and note the topics that interest you most.
  2. Email the lab director, Arpan Mahara, at amahara@purdue.edu with a brief introduction, your background, and the direction you are interested in.
  3. Include a CV or résumé and, if available, links to relevant coursework, projects, or code.
